Top 10 Charleston Male Entertainers

Last updated: October 2026

The best male entertainment for Charleston bachelorette parties, birthdays, and girls nights: private shows at your rental, a male revue, in-home dancers, a touring show, popular show themes, and picks for crews celebrating in other cities.

1
Michael At Your Place
Private Shows
Charleston

Taking the top spot is Michael, Charleston's top-rated private entertainer, with 4.9 stars on Google. He brings an acrobatic, Vegas-style show to your rental, hotel, or home for bachelorette parties, birthdays, and girls nights. Shows are $200 for up to 90 minutes, paid on arrival by cash, Venmo, or Cash App, with brunch or late-night times and last-minute dates welcome.

Gents of Charleston
Male Revue
Charleston

A 90-minute, Vegas-style male revue with choreographed dancing and acrobatics, built for bachelorette and birthday groups. It's the sister show of Music City Gents in Nashville. A great pick if your group wants a full stage-style production with multiple performers. Check their Batch listing for upcoming dates.
